About these numbers

All records are synthetic, in USD. Revenue is net of line-level discounts. Gross profit subtracts product cost only; it excludes tax, shipping and overhead. Orders count distinct order IDs. Average order value divides revenue by distinct orders. YoY uses the same quarter, region, category and channel filters one year earlier. No 2023 data is available. Active customers placed at least one matching order. A repeat customer placed two or more matching orders within the selected period; this is not a retention metric.
